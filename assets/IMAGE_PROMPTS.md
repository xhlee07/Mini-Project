# CTFLY Neon Arena artwork

Created with the built-in **image_gen** tool; no external API key or CLI was used. Generated outputs were copied into this project. The application renders the photos as backgrounds with live Tkinter text and controls above them.

## neon_arena.png

Final saved path: `Mini-Project/assets/neon_arena.png`.

Final prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: high-end gaming internet café desktop app background / wide hero artwork, landscape 1536x1024. Primary request: a cinematic, exceptionally polished photograph of a futuristic premium esports internet café at night. Rows of powerful glass-sided gaming PCs and 240Hz monitors, racing-style chairs, clean industrial black architecture, tasteful electric cyan LED lines, deep ultraviolet and violet light, subtle atmospheric haze, precise reflections on a matte dark floor. Camera looking diagonally down a row of rigs, the visually interesting illuminated machines mostly on the right two thirds; the left third is deep nearly black blue negative space for UI typography. Realistic luxury gaming lounge rather than spaceship, elegant and immersive. Restrained cyan/violet, crisp detail, high contrast but dark overall. No people, no readable monitor content, no brand logos, no letters, no watermark. Artwork only, not a UI mockup.
